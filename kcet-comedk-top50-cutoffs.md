## How to read this document

- **Rank** is the cutoff merit rank a candidate needed to be allotted that seat — **lower rank number = more competitive** (harder to get). A rank of 200 is far more competitive than a rank of 20,000.
- **"Tightened"** means the cutoff rank got lower (more competitive) between rounds; **"loosened"** means it got higher (more room, easier to get in). Movement is shown between each exam's two most recent rounds: KCET Round 1 → Round 2 Mock, and ComedK Mock Round → Round 1.
- **Composite rank position** (used to order every college here) is *not* simply "the college with the single best cutoff." It blends the **median cutoff of every branch category a college offers**, weighted by how often each category is a student's top choice system-wide (from actual 1st-choice cutoff patterns). A college with strong, broad-based demand across popular categories (e.g. CS) ranks ahead of one whose only standout is a single low-demand niche branch. This is the same method the live Dashboard calls "Composite score."
- **Merged rank** in this document combines KCET and ComedK standing: a college qualifies for the Top 50 if it ranks well in *either* exam (its merged position = the better of its two per-exam positions). KCET and ComedK evaluate different, differently-sized applicant pools, so their raw composite scores are not directly comparable — only each list's own ordinal position is used to merge.
- **College name matching** between the two exams (which use unrelated code systems) is done by normalized-name matching, verified to be conservative — a college with no confirmed match on the other exam shows "not matched" rather than risking a wrong pairing.
- **Branch categories:** Computer Science & IT, Electronics & Communication, Electrical & Electronics, Mechanical & Allied, Civil & Construction, Chemical & Polymer, Biotechnology & Life Sciences, Aerospace, Other Branches.
- **Duplicate-looking names:** KEA sometimes lists one physical college under more than one KCET code, each with its own cutoffs (likely different seat-matrix groupings — this data does not say which). Where that happens, both are kept as separate entries and disambiguated with their code, e.g. "B M S College of Engineering (KCET E003)" vs "(KCET E048)".
